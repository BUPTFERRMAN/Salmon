Salmon

Reconstruct the past, not just predict the future.

Salmon is a multi-agent workspace for case replay, historical backtracing, and causal-chain analysis. It turns documents and raw clues into structured evidence through staged parsing, graph construction, reverse reasoning, and synthesis, so you can inspect how an outcome came to be.

Why Salmon Reverse-First, Evidence-Constrained

Built for reconstruction rather than prediction: generate competing explanations, expose key turning points, and keep uncertainty visible.
